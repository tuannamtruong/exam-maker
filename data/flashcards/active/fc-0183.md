# Front
EFA vs ENA — when to use which?

# Back
ENA (Elastic Network Adapter): standard high-throughput VPC networking, up to 100+ Gbps for general workloads. EFA (Elastic Fabric Adapter): adds OS-bypass for HPC and tightly-coupled MPI/ML workloads needing low latency and high inter-node throughput.
