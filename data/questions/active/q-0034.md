# Scenario
A company is configuring DNS for its domain.

# Question
Which DNS record type is most commonly used to verify domain ownership?

# Options
1. MX
2. NS
3. A
4. AAAA
5. TXT

# Explanation
TXT records hold arbitrary text and are the de facto standard for proving domain ownership — services (ACM, Google, Microsoft, etc.) ask you to publish a token in a TXT record they can look up.
