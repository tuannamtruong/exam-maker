# Scenario
deployment to two particular regions and wants to implement a strict tagging requirement.

# Question
Why the other is wrong?

# Options
1. Utilize Service Catalog and create products with approved CloudFormation templates.
2. Utilize approved CloudFormation templates and launch CloudFormation StackSets.

# Explanation
2. StackSets manage deployments across accounts and regions but do not enforce tagging or region restrictions. They do not typically provide governance to prevent non-compliant implementations.
